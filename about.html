HOW IT STARTED

From a simple idea to your favourite corner.

Aurelia started with one simple idea: create a café where people could slow down.

We wanted a place where the coffee was taken seriously without making the experience feel complicated.

Today, Aurelia is a neighbourhood space for morning coffee, afternoon conversations, creative work and everything in between.
